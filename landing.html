Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are the answers?

100% grounded in the college knowledge base. No hallucinations. If information isn't available, the agent refuses to answer.

Is it really local and fast?

Yes! All processing happens on-device with no cloud dependency. P50 latency is ~80ms, P100 <150ms.

Can I deploy this at my college?

Absolutely! The pipeline is completely modular. Just update the knowledge base and deploy.

What about privacy?

All queries stay on-device. No external APIs, no cloud storage, no tracking.
